The contradiction between the SNC-only pilot and EXPERIMENTAL_BASELINE 10.1/10.2 is resolved.

============================================================
1. SNC-ONLY PILOT — APPROVED
============================================================

EXPERIMENTAL_BASELINE now explicitly supersedes the old per-system pilot.

Before CRITERIA.md:
- SNC only;
- at least five fixed-setting repetitions;
- CV_throughput_SNC recorded;
- CV_p99_SNC recorded;
- offered-load sweep for the p99 objective;
- no competitor performance observed.

This is methodologically consistent with preregistration.

============================================================
2. NOISE FLOOR N — APPROVED
============================================================

The rule is now:

N = max(
    3 x max(CV_throughput_SNC, CV_p99_SNC),
    N_MIN
)

N_MIN is selected during the formal criteria-setting review after the SNC pilot and before any competitor performance run.

The final N and N_MIN are written into CRITERIA.md before comparative testing.

Approved.

============================================================
3. COMPETITOR VARIANCE V_MAX — APPROVED
============================================================

V_MAX is fixed in CRITERIA.md before competitor performance.

During comparative repetitions:
- competitor CV <= V_MAX -> cell may be valid;
- competitor CV > V_MAX -> cell is INVALID/TOO NOISY;
- correct the environment and/or rerun;
- N does not grow;
- materiality thresholds do not move.

This prevents competitor noise from changing the decision boundary after results are visible.

Approved.

============================================================
4. P99 OBJECTIVE — APPROVED IN PRINCIPLE
============================================================

The old “slower system at 50% of its own peak” rule is gone.

The objective now comes entirely from the SNC pilot:
- sweep SNC offered load;
- identify stable knee / operating region;
- use SNC's p99 at a documented fraction of stable peak load;
- knee rule and fraction are written before the sweep;
- final p99 objective goes into CRITERIA.md before competitor performance.

Competitors are then measured against the same fixed objective.

Approved.

IMPORTANT FOR CP-1A:
the knee detector/rule must be deterministic enough that two people given the same pilot data select the same knee/objective. Do not leave “stable knee” as a visual judgment.

The pilot plan must define the exact algorithm before the R640 sweep.
