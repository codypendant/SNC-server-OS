11. Pilot noise rule: 5 repetitions and N=3×larger CV is acceptable as a pre-registered noise/materiality guard, NOT a statistical confidence interval. If bare-metal variance is unexpectedly high/non-normal, revise pilot methodology BEFORE viewing cross-system results.

12. MODIFY THE 2× MATERIALITY RULE. Keep 2× as a STRONG PERFORMANCE WIN, not the only possible material win.

Pre-register two paths:

A. STRONG PERFORMANCE WIN:
>=2× primary throughput/core at matched p99 objective, beyond noise, without a material tail/complexity regression.

OR

B. MULTI-DIMENSIONAL WIN:
a smaller but still substantial primary-metric advantage beyond noise (exact threshold fixed from pilot BEFORE comparison) PLUS at least one independent material advantage such as memory/object, p99.9, recovery/failover, network/storage amplification, or operational component count, with no critical dimension materially worse.

Do not permit subjective post-hoc scoring.

13. GO rule: do not require operational complexity to be literally “no worse.” Inventory it as a cost. GO requires enough durable-profile advantage across a realistic workload/locality region to justify that cost.

14. NARROW rule is accepted. If SNC wins only at >=95% locality, one workload, Profile C, or one hardware class, narrow the claim/product rather than pretending it is general.

15. REFINE: maximum two bounded rescue rounds. Each names one measured bottleneck and reruns only affected cells under unchanged criteria. A third rescue means NARROW or STOP.

16. STOP rule accepted using the modified materiality definition: no meaningful durable-profile advantage, advantage disappears under realistic coordination, an unfixable declared-contract failure, or competitor parity with materially lower complexity.
