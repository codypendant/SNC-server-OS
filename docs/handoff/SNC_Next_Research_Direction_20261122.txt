SNC — Next Research and Testing Direction
(cody, project thread, 2026-11-22 02:09:34 UTC; written with Codex. Filed verbatim.)

The owner is reconsidering the long-term choice between the original full state-native operating system and the focused agent runtime.
Do not prematurely settle this architectural question.
Our next objective is evidence, not another major implementation commitment.
Track A — Agent Runtime
Finish PR #185's G2 verification gaps first.
Then prepare a narrowly scoped comparative evaluation against:

* DBOS Transact
* Restate, including its Rust SDK and virtual objects
* Temporal
* LangGraph

Use equivalent observable guarantees and idiomatic implementations. Compare recovery, persistent memory, tool-effect safety, decision reconstruction, durable events, operational complexity and performance.
Do not use missing SNC-specific schema fields as evidence that competing systems cannot provide equivalent functionality.
Track B — Original State-Native OS
Preserve the original SNC architecture and research.
Identify two decisive benchmarks testing whether state-native object execution and scheduling provide meaningful advantages over established infrastructure.
Use competent baselines, equivalent durability and explicit performance thresholds.
Do not resume custom replication or begin raft-rs integration merely to perform these comparisons.
Decision criteria
Report separately:

1. What existing systems already solve.
2. What SNC demonstrably improves.
3. What SNC provides that competitors cannot readily reproduce.
4. Engineering effort required to deliver each direction.
5. Whether the observed benefits justify additional implementation complexity.

Keep G3 on HOLD pending the completed G2 review and separate authorization.
Prepare a bounded comparison plan for owner and Codex approval before expanding implementation.
The goal is to find the strongest technical and commercial direction for SNC, whether that ultimately means a full operating system, an agent runtime, an SDK, or a hybrid.
