PRE-CRITERIA SNC PILOT:

CRITERIA.md then fixes:

    3 x max(CV_throughput_SNC, CV_p99_SNC),

COMPETITOR VARIANCE VALIDITY:

CRITERIA.md also fixes V_MAX.

During comparative repetitions:
- competitor CV <= V_MAX: cell valid;
- competitor CV > V_MAX: cell INVALID/TOO NOISY and must be rerun or environment corrected;
- do not enlarge N after seeing competitor variance;
- do not weaken materiality thresholds.

N is a preregistered materiality/noise guard, not a per-competitor confidence interval.

Do not choose numeric N_MIN or V_MAX tonight.
