PR #44 / S1 + EXPERIMENTAL ROADMAP REVIEW

DECISION

PR #43 Experimental Validation roadmap: APPROVED.
PR #44 S1 bounded destination staging: APPROVED.

Do NOT start S2/S3 or major platform features.

S1 achieved its primary goal. On the corrected 100K shared-key workload, the longest non-preemptible destination task fell from about 2979 ms to about 3.8 ms. STATUS max during final staging fell from about 2986 ms to 16 ms, and foreground deposit max fell from roughly 1.6 s to about 32 ms.

The tradeoff is real and must remain visible: total move time increased about 45%, while client-visible E_MIGRATING increased about 37% on shared-email and about 10% on unique-email workloads. This is exactly the kind of tradeoff the falsification program must evaluate.

Accepted S1 refinements:
- non-final STAGE also uses bounded units;
- reply slot_report is bounded without changing reply content;
- driver resend covers final and non-final stages;
- MOVE ROLE replaces expensive INFO probing;
- STAGE is its own bounded unit;
- no new wire, durable, placement or migration protocol;
- volatile FINALIZING remains non-authoritative;
- D1/D7 fail-closed proof remains intact;
- source/destination failover, driver death, retry, fencing and existing migration crash tests pass;
- --move-units off remains rollback/comparison.

The shared-email index path is now measured at roughly 0.2 ms per put. It is not itself a multi-second primitive in this workload. Do not optimize the index now.

Final verification:
- RelWithDebInfo 22/22;
- ASan+UBSan 22/22;
- TSan 22/22.
The sanitizer S1 scale was reduced because sanitizer overhead exceeded the driver's deadline; that is valid correctness coverage, not scale evidence.
